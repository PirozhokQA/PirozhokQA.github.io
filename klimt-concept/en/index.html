Before you book

Useful answers for your visit.

How do I arrange a visit?+

Call us or prepare a message here and send it to Klimt on Instagram. Our administrator will confirm the treatment and time.

Can I choose a specialist?+

Yes. Include their name in your request. Our administrator will check their schedule.

When will I know the final price?+

Published prices provide a guide. Confirm the price, products and what is included before your visit.

Can I bring a reference?+

Yes. Select a portfolio image using “I would like this look”, or share your own reference with our administrator. Your specialist will discuss how it can work for you.

How do I reschedule or cancel?+

Contact our administrator by phone or on Instagram as early as possible. Include your name, treatment and confirmed appointment time.

Do I need to prepare?+

Preparation depends on the treatment. Ask your specialist in advance about cosmetology, ELOS or lash extension appointments.

Where is the salon and when are you open?+

17 Gyurjyan Street, Yerevan. Open every day, 10:00–20:00. Get directions in the “Your first visit” section.
